About

My career started on the ramp as an aircraft technician, after a B.Tech in Aeronautical Engineering. I then moved into maintenance data: digitising work orders and work packages, and tracking AD/SB and life-limited parts data in a relational database. As a Business Analyst at Laminaar Aviation Infotech I wrote BRDs and functional specifications, ran Scrum and Kanban delivery with airline SMEs, and led UAT and release testing for maintenance, logistics and procurement modules. Since 2023 I have worked on the airline side, in supply chain at United Airlines, evaluating PMA and OOPP alternate parts, validating technical effectivity against manufacturer documentation, and building Power BI dashboards and Foundry SQL queries for stakeholders. Across these roles the common thread is turning operational and technical data into decisions that engineering, planning and procurement teams can act on.

Domain knowledge is presented as working experience with aviation ERP processes. This site does not claim a maintenance licence or airworthiness authority.
